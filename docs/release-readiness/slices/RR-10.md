Public uploads are now capped at the type limits (images/audio 20 MiB; video/documents 25 MiB) with base64/source-path prechecks and fail-closed scanning. Large upload/streaming-scanner support is not claimed. **RR10-I02 parent-delete authorization:** `forum_user` now receives only the topic/thread delete permissions needed to attempt deletion of its own content; exact user/profile/scope/readability ownership checks remain in the forum service.
