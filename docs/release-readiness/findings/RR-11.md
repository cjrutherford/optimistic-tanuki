## Findings

### RR11-F01 — Profile deletion has no supported lifecycle service contract

**Severity:** P0 risk candidate — the bounded fail-closed source repair and no-write authorization evidence are accepted; release remains blocked pending a supported lifecycle service. No vulnerability or successful deletion has been reproduced.
**Evidence:** The RR-11 source audit found that `apps/gateway/src/controllers/profile/profile.controller.ts` forwarded a bare ID after `profile.delete`, without comparing the authenticated owner to the target, and that `apps/profile/src/profiles/profiles.controller.ts` has no `ProfileCommands.Delete` handler. RR11-I01 now looks up the target with `ProfileCommands.Get`, binds `UserDetails.userId` to `ProfileDto.userId`, requires the exact request app scope, returns 403 for foreign/unknown owner, wrong scope, global or null-scope target, 404 for a missing target, generic 502 for lookup failure, and 501 for an owned app-scoped target while lifecycle deletion is unavailable. The verifier reports authenticated HTTP statuses of 501 for the owned app-scoped target, 403 for foreign owner, wrong app scope and same-user global target, and 404 for missing target. The harness found exact equality of parsed profile JSON objects before and after the requests. Null-scope 403 and generic 502 are covered by unit tests only; verifier provenance, cleanup, and receipt are recorded in [RR11-I01](./RR-11-I01.md), receipt SHA-256 `3086ae2614bba86810ca21f359f6cb2ecbdb952a8d3a4e9b58abecf7c516422b`. The live RPC stream was not instrumented; source inspection and unit assertions confirm the gateway does not send `ProfileCommands.Delete`. Focused tests cover these branches and existing profile read/update tests pass; see [RR11-I01](./RR-11-I01.md). The retained-profile test at `apps/client-interface-e2e/src/rr09-i04-own-post-edit.spec.ts` preserves the synthetic global profile. RR-03 independently records the broader lifecycle gap.
**Impact:** The accepted source contract and five-case authenticated HTTP matrix fail closed. The supported profile-service handler and dependent-data retention/deletion behavior remain absent; posts, assets, permission assignments, and activity have no approved lifecycle rule or readback evidence. Profile removal remains distinct from account deletion, which also spans credentials, tokens, key data, OAuth providers, and email-action tokens.
**Required evidence:** The accepted I01 checkpoint and its provenance, cleanup, and receipt are recorded in [RR11-I01](./RR-11-I01.md). The live matrix did not exercise null-scope profiles or runtime lookup failure; those paths have source/unit evidence only. Keep RR09-I04's synthetic global profile intact. A successful owner deletion and dependent-record readback must wait until a supported service handler and approved retention rule exist. Map account-level deletion separately across authentication credentials/tokens, key data, OAuth providers, and email-action tokens.

### RR11-F02 — Public legal, privacy, support, and account lifecycle information is not reachable from OT or TS

**Severity:** P1, with legal/product decisions pending.
**Evidence:** Both route tables expose landing, authentication, and product pages but no privacy/terms/support/export/delete routes. The OT landing footer contains only the product name and tagline. The TS landing ends after its workflow steps without legal or support links. The TS account page covers profile, communities, and preferences; it has no deletion or export action. OT settings include block, mute, and report controls, but no support contact or account lifecycle process.
**Impact:** A visitor cannot find the applicable rules, privacy explanation, support or abuse-contact path, or a supported way to request account deletion/export. Existing moderation/report features do not establish a staffed contact route or handling process.
**Required decisions/evidence:** Product owner supplies the intended contact route, operational owner and response process; legal review supplies or approves public terms/privacy language. Document deletion/export handling and verify the links from both public roots. Do not invent legal policy as an implementation shortcut.

### RR11-F03 — Cookie and third-party resource behavior needs a user-facing disclosure

**Severity:** P1.
**Evidence:** `apps/gateway/src/controllers/authentication/authentication.controller.ts` sets browser sessions as HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax cookies with a one-hour max age; `AUTH_COOKIE_SECURE` controls Secure, defaulting on in production but can be overridden. OT stores profile-selection/profile-list state in local storage (`auth-state.service.ts`); session credentials are removed from the legacy local-storage key. Unauthenticated production landing loads showed no cookies before login. Fresh browser navigation requested Highlight.js from cdnjs on OT, Google Fonts from Google stylesheet/font hosts on both products, and Picsum images on TS. Both roots sent public `POST /api/performance/rum` beacons (201); source (`libs/common-ui/src/lib/common-ui/performance-monitor.ts`) records app ID, query-stripped/normalized pathname, render mode, and performance metric names/values/timestamps. Numeric, UUID, and over-80-character path segments are normalized; other path segments remain. Gateway aggregates telemetry in in-memory windows. No Google Analytics or other third-party analytics SDK appeared in this navigation-only run.
**Impact:** Session, browser storage, third-party requests, and performance telemetry are not explained to users. Public performance telemetry is verified; pathname segments may contain product identifiers and were not tested across authenticated/private routes. Authenticated cookie issuance, resolved production cookie flags, telemetry retention beyond in-memory process state, all app flows, and any other runtime tracking were NOT RUN. The telemetry evidence does not establish collection of message or post content.
**Required evidence:** Publish an accurate notice covering essential session storage, profile preferences, third-party resources, and verified performance telemetry; reconcile session settings and telemetry retention with operator-confirmed production configuration. Do not claim analytics or nonessential cookies are absent from this bounded source/browser check.

Production also displayed counts of 289 localities, 426 communities, and 1 member. Their provenance was not verified; they are recorded as unverified UI values, not as independently proven false or as a standalone P0. No production source/bundle SHA was established. No “free forever” pricing promise appeared in the reviewed TS production root.
**Impact:** The production deployment does not match the accepted TS source copy/dispositions. The previously withdrawn moderation/reputation, fundraising/donation, and paid-tier claims make this a release-blocking truth-in-marketing issue. Production checkout, donor handling, paid entitlements, support service levels, and business sample/count provenance remain unverified. This observation does not prove that money was collected or that displayed counts are false.
**Required evidence:** RR-01 must reconcile the actual deployed TS content to approved dispositions and verify production serves the reviewed early-access bundle. Keep withdrawn claims out of current-availability copy. Any retained donation/tier claims require accepted payment, entitlement, support, and funds-handling evidence; until then use RR-01's RETRACT/DEFER disposition. Preserve proof of deployment/bundle identity in RR-01/RR-12.
