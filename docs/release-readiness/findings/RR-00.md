## Product intent and release contract

The owner supplied the audit and philosophy as this branch's intended release program. RR-00 preserves their substantive product definitions:

- OT: "You are the social network"; optional communities; primary feeds honor user choices; discovery stays separate; privacy is an audience contract; chat/feed/forum support different tempos; no fabricated adoption claims.
- TS: organized around place; locality relationships need not mean strict residency; public browsing, local exchange/classifieds, and messaging form its core; Daylight requires separate acceptance.
- HAI: sibling products use the existing ecosystem tag; shared-login claims require evidence, without redundant persistent navigation.
- Release standard: advertised behavior works, private content stays private, critical workflows survive ordinary use, and users can understand the product.

This is conformity to the owner-supplied specification, not a claim that the implementations already conform. Runtime acceptance remains with RR-01 onward. Product-policy changes or philosophy exceptions require an explicit documented decision/ADR rather than silent reinterpretation.
