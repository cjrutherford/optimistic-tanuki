# D01 — Authentication deletion owner

Status: **VERIFIED LOCAL OT/TS OWNER ADAPTER** (2026-10-24). D02 is next. Full account deletion remains incomplete.

The Authentication owner accepts only a current signed `authentication` grant for its exact fence, erase or readback action. It installs the proven account/profile identity fences, revokes sessions, removes OAuth linkage and email-action credentials, and purges export artifacts before removing their job rows. A missing Profile scope permits early account credential revocation but returns an explicit scope-required result rather than a completed owner fence.

Artifact purge transactions carry the validated deletion operation context. Candidate queries fetch bounded job IDs; a failed store purge retains the descriptor and cleanup-pending state for retry. Erase repeats purge and checks locked job metadata before deleting jobs. Counts and the retained root identity must read back correctly; malformed/missing results cannot become empty success.

Own account tokens and orphan tokens tied to captured profiles are removed. A token naming another account is preserved even when its profile binding conflicts; that conflict leaves a nonzero readback and blocks completeness. Shared guardian records retain history and the counterpart's identifiers while the deleting account's role identifiers, email/DOB and unsafe consent payloads are cleared. Unbound guardian email-only rows remain explicitly unresolved. Root user fields, keys, the operation ledger and permanent fence remain for D26 and dependent ownership proof.
