## Changes

- OT: remove adoption and permanent-ownership claims; lead with “You are the social network”; explain optional communities, a distinct Explore surface, intended explicit audiences, and the main forum. Label early-stage/early-access behavior rather than presenting unaccepted guarantees.
- TS: lead with localities, communities, classifieds, and messaging in early access. Remove moderation/reputation, fundraising, paid business promotion, misleading totals, and unverified resident figures. Keep the existing locality carousel and public navigation.
- Metadata: OT gets a product title and early-access description; TS description explicitly identifies early access.
- Landing loading: communities load independently of payments; unavailable-data messaging is distinct from a genuinely empty locality set. Membership rules, public/private authorization, and locality eligibility are unchanged.
- Deferred register: retain DF-001–DF-005 and add source/validation distinctions for reputation, financial features, and withdrawn permanence/portability claims.
