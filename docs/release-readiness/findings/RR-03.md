## Operator email evidence and invite transport

The operator confirmed verification email delivery works and reported that invites only log. These are different senders: authentication receives `SMTP_*` environment variables; community and project invite mailers run in the gateway. Gateway provider selection is already environment-controlled, but Compose omitted its SMTP variables, forcing its console fallback even when `.env` configured SMTP for authentication. Compose now forwards `SMTP_HOST`, `SMTP_PORT`, `SMTP_SECURE`, `SMTP_USER`, `SMTP_PASS`, and `SMTP_FROM` to the gateway. Transport remains environment-controlled; no per-app delivery setting was introduced. Registry metadata supplies product identity and invitation destinations.

This explains the checked-in Compose behavior; actual production gateway environment and invite receipt have not been inspected. Operator-confirmed verification delivery is recorded separately from independently verified lifecycle evidence. Invite creation/claim/receipt belongs to RR-05 (OT) and RR-06 (TS); delivery still needs an operator recheck after deployment of the environment wiring.

## Account model and evidence boundaries

The operator confirmed shared account credentials with separate sign-ins for OT and TS. Production roots are separate domains; gateway session cookies are host-only, HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax, path `/`. Same-host development ports share a cookie namespace, so cross-app browser validation uses `127.0.0.1` for OT and `localhost` for TS to model distinct hostnames.

Both apps expose `/auth/verify`, `/auth/magic-link`, and `/auth/reset-password` through shared email-auth routes. Login exposes email-auth entry points. Source presence and mocked service tests establish neither email delivery nor token-consumption acceptance. Authentication tests cover email reset revocation with mocked persistence; the old-password reset command does not revoke existing tokens and needs a documented account-security policy and real lifecycle evidence.

## Development stack limitations
