Ordinary asset uploads now persist a reservation before writing bytes, hold a row lock through storage and lease validation, and compensate failed activation. Failed compensation leaves a cleanup record. Startup cleanup reclaims expired reservations after process death. Notification cancellation clears queued timers and waits for started writes; pending notifications remain deliberately volatile. Export-job cancellation clears the current lease and blocks stale completion. Guardian-notice suppression waits behind a sending row and preserves provider-accepted state. Mail transports have bounded connection/response controls; SMTP socket timeouts do not establish an absolute send deadline.
