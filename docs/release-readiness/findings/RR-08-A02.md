# RR08-A02 — Shared compose and private attachment audiences

**Date:** 2026-10-07. **Baseline:** `88e6e227f`. **Status:** ACCEPTED — bounded repair. Full RR-08 remains open.

## Scope and design

Close the scheduled shared compose/social/forum attachment audience gate. At baseline, shared uploads and the OT feed callback omitted audience context; asset reads resolved only classified legacy references and persisted community audiences. Live confirmation and exclusions are recorded below.

Respect the owning policies: social private-community content uses current approved membership, follower-only posts use current follow relationships, and private forum topics/threads/replies inherit profile-owner access. Community membership is not a substitute for forum or follower policy.

Extend durable asset restrictions with typed owner/follower policies alongside A01 community associations. Bind context to the verified session and owned profile; authoritative services establish scope/access. Never trust submitted visibility or identity as authorization. Protect private uploads before saving, retain restrictions after reference removal, and validate attachment compatibility before widening content audiences. Resolve legacy social/forum HTML references through owning services, accepting private associations only for the persisted asset owner. Preserve public-image positives and the internal-media signed assertion boundary. Reject unknown policies and fail closed on service errors.

Owners: assets/gateway/social/forum, shared constants/models, compose-lib/social-ui/forum-ui, OT feed upload callback, focused client-interface E2E. Root owns release reports and shared-stack preparation; one worker owns implementation. No worktree, reset, seed or original key replacement.
