# R2-02 — Ninety-day activity and history retention

Status: **VERIFIED LOCAL**, 2026-10-27. Classification: **COMPLETE-local**.
Scope: OT `client-interface`, TS `local-hub` and required shared owners.

The reconciled [inventory](../evidence/df-007/r2-retention-inventory.json) contains
eleven database sources: Profile and Assets guardian activity; Social activity,
profile views, search history, membership audit, moderation and guardian content
audit; Forum moderation audit; Auth guardian policy notices; and terminal export
job history. It also explicitly reconciles the five non-ORM logging boundaries.

A shared metadata-backed runner starts with each owning service, removes at
most 200 rows per source/pass, and schedules bounded retries. It uses the database
clock and a precise inclusive ninety-day cutoff, including microseconds and
UTC timestamp-without-zone handling. Redacted audit history has the same age
rule. Failed or locked rows remain visible through independent readback.
The runner never bypasses deletion fences.

Export history is pruned in batches of 25 only after artifact custody is clear,
with no live lease or cleanup/assembly intent. Unresolved private descriptors
remain discoverable and overdue history remains pending. Active queued/running
operations and the accepted deidentified permanent-fence ledger have explicit
operational dispositions; they are not exceptions retaining activity history.

Real PostgreSQL tests passed exact/older/newer microsecond rows across all nine
Profile/Assets/Social/Forum sources, deletion failure and retry, fenced history,
approved deidentification and later aging. The live replica fixture independently
confirmed persisted Social history removed on startup, aged Auth notices removed,
terminal history pruning and unresolved custody reported pending. Counterpart
records remained intact. Current owning builds and lint passed.

The operator confirmed no retained legacy/manual OT/TS logs and no forwarded
collector. Current diagnostic boundaries are payload-free. Future
[OpenTelemetry/Grafana setup](../../operators/ot-ts-observability.md) still needs
its own cleanup/readback qualification. No collector was installed.

See the [receipt](../evidence/df-007/checkpoint-r2-02.json) and
[operator notes](../../operators/ot-ts-retention.md). This accepts the declared
local environment, not external sinks, backups or another deployment.
