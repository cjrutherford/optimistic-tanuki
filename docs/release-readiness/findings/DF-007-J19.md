# DF-007-J19 — ProfileView attribution and private analytics

**Status:** locally verified at the bounded private runtime scope; DF-007 and RR-11 remain open.

J19 binds newly recorded view actors to the authenticated account, permits analytics reads only for currently owned profiles, and removes counterpart identifiers from recent-view projections. The source capture is based on `4c5d8661781e652d8b7c76c6c47d71e9e8ccf6ae` with six frozen source files and manifest `8ae75f8bcf6126d2a3fb5232ab9d3d29cef87e575d180eede3d509e4b572bb1f`. All 43 protected paths were excluded and rechecked.

## Behavior and evidence

`POST /api/profile-analytics/view` takes `viewerId` from the authenticated account UUID, even when a legacy body field attempts to spoof it. Recording a view of another profile remains allowed. Both analytics GET routes use Profile's narrow account/profile ownership RPC, so an account can read its secondary profiles while foreign and missing targets fail before Social. The private test also moved the selected profile's current owner after signing and confirmed a still-valid token claim did not authorize the stale ownership.

Social acknowledges writes and returns timestamp-only recent-view records, including to direct internal RPC callers. Summary counts and the newest-100 source summary remain; source labels are limited to `direct`, `profile`, `search`, `feed` and `other`, with unknown historical labels projected as `other`. Recent lists default to 10 and are bounded to 100. Uppercase legacy profile UUID strings remain queryable. Historical viewer IDs are not rewritten or treated as verified ownership; their export disposition remains J23/J24.

## Limits and next work

Internal service calls remain a trusted TCP boundary. Ownership RPC waits are configured at three seconds; malformed replies and mocked failures have unit evidence, while timer expiry and a live outage were not tested. This private harness does not establish a full Gateway deployment, browser journey, external-provider flow, remote CI result or production qualification. Fixture row counts do not prove query cancellation or byte-level retention. Timestamp evidence follows the UTC private runtime's existing timezone-free column convention; timezone portability is not established.

No historical attribution was repaired and no consent is inferred. Export handling for historical viewer identifiers remains in J23/J24. J20 Presence audience binding is next; DF-007 and RR-11 remain open.
