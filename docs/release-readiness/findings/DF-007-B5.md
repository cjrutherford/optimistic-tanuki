# DF-007 B5: bounded Social personal-state preview

**Result: locally verified, bounded internal preview (2026-10-18).** B5 extends the existing Social account-rights participant with five actor-owned personal-state projections. It does not complete DF-007 or RR-11 and does not provide a full Social/account inventory, portable export/download, or deletion workflow.

The added projections are `SavedItem`, `SearchHistory`, outgoing `FollowEntity`, outgoing `UserBlock`, and outgoing `UserMute`. Together with B4's posts, comments, reactions, votes, attachments, and Poll definitions, the preview returns eleven partial arrays. Saved/search entries are restricted to validated owned profile IDs; relationship rows include only outgoing actor-side records (`followerId`, `blockerId`, `muterId`). Incoming relationship records are not queried. Counterpart and target identifiers are opaque references. The projection omits counterpart content/profile data, `FollowEntity.isMutual`, `SavedItem.itemTitle`, and `SearchHistory.resultCount`.

The participant retains B4's 15-second read budget and selected-column queries. Each of the five added stores uses pages of at most 100 and a ceiling of 1,000 scanned rows plus one truncation probe. This scanned-row bound does not extend to B4's existing collection readers. Rows are rechecked against the exact actor/profile scope. Invalid legacy mute durations are omitted and keep the result partial; valid durations are null or a positive safe integer no greater than 31,536,000 seconds. The result remains always partial and unfenced: live identity pages and dataset reads do not prove a complete inventory. String and serialized-output caps do not bound database-read memory, and the response timer does not establish cancellation of underlying SQL.
