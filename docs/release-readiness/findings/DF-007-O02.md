App Configurator is the ninth owner in the leased account-export job, after Workspace. Its three sources return bounded registration and personal-membership receipts selected by the authenticated account and captured current Profile identities. Configuration capture also validates the composite parent and recorded owner pair. Membership in someone else's app returns only the subject's own receipt; counterpart owners, other members and configuration content are excluded.
