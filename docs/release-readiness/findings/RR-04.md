## Repair order and release gate

1. **Completed narrow repair:** remove the anonymous bypass and preserve authenticated reporting callers. Reviewable together; not sufficient for promotion.
2. **Shared privacy repair:** enforce post audience AND community access consistently for REST, comments, search/trending, personalized feeds, and realtime recipients. Approved membership is required where applicable; follow alone cannot grant private-community access. Include privacy changes/revocation and active-client cache behavior. Reuse RR-05/RR-06 membership cases and RR-08 safety cases rather than implementing separate privacy rules per slice.
3. **Publishing and sharing repair:** expose and validate an explicit audience, choose a safe community default, and check source access/audience intersections on share creation and reads. Assess legacy default-public community posts before changing semantics; any required schema/default migration follows the owning Nx migration target.
4. **Following-feed and acceptance repair:** use profile identity consistently, share the same read policy, and run chronology/tab/pagination, deletion, notification, and complete multi-user browser gates.
