## Feed and remaining acceptance evidence

Source inspection shows descending `createdAt` for the websocket initial feed, HTTP public-feed requests, and personalized/community feed. Explore is a separate route/surface; trending uses a separate query. No engagement ranking was found in those primary-feed paths. Runtime ordering, vote/engagement invariance, tab isolation, pagination, follow controls in the browser, and Explore navigation remain **VERIFY**. Socket replacement of feed state and independent feed predicates require parity checks rather than assuming one chronological query establishes the whole experience.

Mention notifications use generic text rather than quoted post content. Other notification kinds, recipient authorization, revoked membership, and previously delivered previews were not exercised. I01 now supplies targeted evidence for deletion across active caches/search/direct links, alternate profiles, and membership suspension/revocation. Shares after restriction/deletion, the full moderation/scheduling/block matrix and integrated acceptance remain I02/I03/RR-08. Existing unit coverage is supporting evidence only.

No core privacy blocker is deferred or waived. Public privacy/audience claims remain VERIFY until these gates pass; bounded F01/I01 repairs must not be described as full RR-04 acceptance.
