# RR11-I03 — Public information surface

**Status:** HOLD — NOT IMPLEMENTED / NOT ACCEPTED.
**Date:** 2026-10-13
**Baseline:** RR-11 findings updated after the conditional RR11-V01 browser checkpoint.
**Scope:** Public policy, privacy/disclosure, support/contact and account-lifecycle information surfaces for OT and TS.

## Disposition

No public routes, shared renderer, footer links, policy text, forms, or contact destinations were added. The I02 manual-approval record remains pending, and the user has confirmed that the policies, contacts, and request processes do not currently exist. The I03 plan explicitly requires approved decisions and operational verification before implementation; publishing empty routes or forms would imply information or processes that are not available.

The current source inventory finds no public policy, terms, support, contact, abuse/copyright, deletion/export, or status route in either app. OT has an authenticated `/settings/privacy` surface for block, mute, and supported-content reports; that is not a public policy page or general request channel. TS account actions are Seller Dashboard, Messages, and Log Out. OT's landing page has a footer without policy links; TS's landing page has no footer. OT and TS route tables have distinct wildcard redirects (`/feed` and `/`, respectively), so later public routes must be explicit.

## Required preconditions and eventual acceptance

I03 may resume after the I02 decision record is explicitly approved and each proposed request/contact destination is operationally verified. Approved content must be factual, product-specific where behavior differs, and match resolved production configuration. The shared UI may render approved content and links but must not invent default legal text, contacts, dates, response times, age rules, or retention periods.

The implementation acceptance matrix remains: direct-load and refresh each approved route signed out and signed in; prove explicit route/fallback behavior; verify every link reaches its approved destination; review keyboard navigation, semantic headings, focus, mobile reflow, and accessibility; compare displayed copy to signed-off decisions; verify OT and TS disclosures remain distinct where required; confirm no new external resources or telemetry fields; and reconcile production bundle identity under RR-01/RR-12. Any deletion/export affordance additionally requires an independently supported and approved process; account deletion/export remains DF-007 unless owner-approved mapping establishes a bounded scope.

## Current evidence and limits

The conditional V01 observation is recorded in [RR11-V01](./RR-11-V01.md). It confirms current public-root content and the absence of policy/contact links in the captured visible snapshots only. It does not accept any I03 requirement. Production bundle identity, authenticated route behavior, resolved production privacy configuration, full network inventory, legal approval, contact delivery, deletion/export handling, and accessibility of future pages remain unverified.

**Result:** keep I03 on HOLD. RR-11 remains GAP FOUND; no release gate is cleared.
