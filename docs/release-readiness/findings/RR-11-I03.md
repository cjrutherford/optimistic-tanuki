## Disposition

The owner approved limited public wording for support, privacy questions, abuse reports, and copyright concerns, with no response-time promise. A shared `/contact` page and footer link were added to OT and TS, using the approved HAI public name, Georgia base, stated US-and-territories service area, and monitored support inbox. The page offers a `mailto:` link only; no form, response target, account deletion/export statement, or completion promise was added. This approval covers this limited wording only. No agent email was sent, and the owner-reported mailbox setup/receipt and monitoring are not independently verified.

Privacy/terms/age-policy disclosures, request-specific handling, deletion/export, outage/status information, production bundle identity, and operational contact evidence remain open. The I02 manual-review record is still pending outside this limited contact wording. RR-11 remains GAP FOUND; this checkpoint does not accept I03 or any legal/process requirement.

The current source inventory finds no public policy, terms, deletion/export, or status route in either app. OT has an authenticated `/settings/privacy` surface for block, mute, and supported-content reports; that is not a public policy page or general request channel. TS account actions are Seller Dashboard, Messages, and Log Out. Both shells render the shared contact footer once; OT keeps its existing tagline and TS uses its own product tagline. `/contact` is an explicit route before each app's wildcard redirect (`/feed` and `/` respectively).

## Required preconditions and eventual acceptance

Remaining I03 content requires its corresponding owner-approved decisions and operational evidence. Approved content must be factual, product-specific where behavior differs, and match resolved production configuration. The shared UI may render approved content and links but must not invent default legal text, contacts, dates, response times, age rules, or retention periods.

The implementation acceptance matrix remains: direct-load and refresh each approved route signed out and signed in; prove explicit route/fallback behavior; verify every link reaches its approved destination; review keyboard navigation, semantic headings, focus, mobile reflow, and accessibility; compare displayed copy to signed-off decisions; verify OT and TS disclosures remain distinct where required; confirm no new external resources or telemetry fields; and reconcile production bundle identity under RR-01/RR-12. Any deletion/export affordance additionally requires an independently supported and approved process; account deletion/export remains DF-007 unless owner-approved mapping establishes a bounded scope.
