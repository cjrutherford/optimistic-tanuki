**Status:** ACCEPTED — bounded RR09-I01 repair. RR-09 remains GAP FOUND; I02, I03, and V01 are still required before RR-10.

**Scope:** Repair the recorded Towne Square `/cities`, `/communities`, `/login`, `/register`, listing-create, and listing-card findings. No shared theme focus behavior (I02), Optimistic Tanuki controls (I03), or unrelated RR-09 acceptance criteria are included.

**Projects and owners touched:** `local-hub`, `classified-ui`, and `client-interface-e2e`. `classified-ui` has a single app consumer, `local-hub`. No services, schemas, migrations, shared theme libraries, or other apps were changed.

## Implemented changes

- `/communities` filter groups wrap within their available width; small-screen groups stack and the community grid constrains its minimum track to the viewport.
- Cities and communities filter chips, community map link, local map reset/zoom controls, and login/register cross-links now meet the project’s 44px target-size goal. This is a usability target; this report does not claim WCAG 2.2 AA target-size conformance.
- The recorded soft locality filter and community metadata use readable foreground text over their subtle token-derived fills. Active locality chips use the paired on-secondary token. Login/register links use the foreground token and an underline. The community map link uses semantic on-primary text.
- Listing form Cancel uses foreground text on its neutral surface. Listing price and actions use the readable primary-text token; status uses readable success/danger text tokens. The listing image input now has a visible associated label and a per-component unique input ID.
- The new opt-in `rr09-i01-towne-square.spec.ts` acceptance spec exercises 320×740, 375×812, 768×1024, 1280×633, and 1440×900 in both actual themes. After each route navigation it operates/verifies the Appearance control and waits for seven successive computed-color samples 50ms apart. Before every contrast decision, it records seven stable samples of the target foreground and ancestor backgrounds.
- Public assertions cover horizontal bounds, active and inactive locality/community-filter contrast, filter/map/auth-link target bounds, and auth-link/map-link contrast. Since the current public stack has no seed localities, the controlled seller/buyer case creates an owned locality with a state-filter chip and checks that dynamic chip’s contrast and target at all five widths and both themes. The same text-only fixture covers file-input labeling and listing form/card contrast; it uploads no media. Cleanup verifies the listing owner record is soft-removed and absent from the buyer’s active community list, and verifies the exact community and city IDs are absent. Controlled accounts are retained, as in the existing RR-09 audit policy.
- The acceptance collector records scoped axe results and screenshots. Axe violations and incomplete results remain recorded evidence for review; the collector does not turn their presence or absence into a full-conformance claim.
