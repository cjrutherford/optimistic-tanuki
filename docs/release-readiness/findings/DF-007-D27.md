Status: VERIFIED LOCAL — OT `client-interface` and TS `local-hub`, 2026-10-26.
Classification: COMPLETE for the bounded local backend slice.

The checked-in private harness boots actual production owner modules against
12 fresh migrated PostgreSQL databases, with real JWT/TCP/HTTP/Socket.IO and
encrypted local files. The passing run started 15 owner processes over its
lifetime, including a killed/restarted coordinator; 14 owner processes and two

replicas, while the other owners exercised singleton endpoints. There were no
fabricated owner acknowledgements or successful fake cleanup adapters.

One full protocol case passed with no skips. It:

- Accepted deletion through the authenticated RPC and discovered/froze profiles
  through the actual Profile owner.
- Held a real verified media read, killed the waiting coordinator, denied both
  app sessions through the surviving Auth replica, and reclaimed the expired
  lease on real process startup. An unexpired old-attempt grant was rejected.
- Blocked on a deliberately unsupported product-scope row while retaining keys.
  The observer then removed that artificial setup row; OT/TS cleanup did not
  erase another product's data.
- Blocked on a real symlink custody fault, retained recoverable state and keys,
  and completed owner erasure after the observer removed the injected symlink.
- Failed Auth deletion after Profile finalization through a real database
  trigger, retained Auth/key state, and recovered successfully after fault removal.
- Preserved counterpart content, identities, shared membership/payment records,
  media hashes, decryptable artifacts, spools, caches and socket connections.
  Owned messages, credentials, media/artifacts and transient copies were absent.
