### RR13-F01 — Internal canonical marketing sources contradict RR-01

**Severity:** P1 — source-of-truth and claim-control gap; not a newly asserted live product defect.

`docs/marketing/README.md:19-24,53-78` directs local-commerce evaluators to a Towne Square donations/sponsorship overview, names Messaging Pillars as the canonical source, and routes campaign-generation prompts through these ground-truth documents. `docs/marketing/messaging-pillars.md:16-24,32-52` says it is the canonical source for outward-facing messaging and ground truth for generated copy, and describes OT as “trustworthy” and “community-owned”; its general TS commerce/civic-participation positioning is not itself a withdrawn feature claim. `docs/marketing/towne-square.md:6-8,21-46` calls the product “active,” targets moderators seeking a “trusted community surface,” and presents donations and sponsorships as workflow proof. `docs/marketing/platform-product-matrix.md:7-18` explicitly presents donations, sponsorships, and transaction flows as existing TS capabilities. Those explicit money, paid-feature, and moderation/reputation assertions conflict with RR-01's `RETRACT` decisions for funding, donation widgets, business tiers, featured placement, analytics, and moderation/reputation; RR-07 still gates any released civic-feed claim. Generic local coordination, classifieds, seller messaging, and civic participation as product direction remain `REWORD`/`VERIFY` under RR-01 and their behavior owners. Because the README and messaging-pillar document direct future campaign generation, these conflicts can reintroduce withdrawn claims even though the app landing source removed them.

**Disposition:** Interim internal source repair completed in [I01](./RR-13-I01.md). The release-readiness gap remains until claim owners confirm the accepted set and RR-12 correlates it to served bundles. No new deferral record was created.

### RR13-F02 — Newcomer orientation and community-steward acquisition are not evidenced

**Severity:** P1 — required RR-13 deliverables are incomplete.

The user can enter through existing account routes, but there is no RR-13 evidence here that a first-time user is oriented toward the next accepted action or that stewards are recruited with responsibilities and boundaries accurately described.

**Disposition:** Keep as an RR-13 deliverable. [I02](./RR-13-I02-DRAFT.md) is internal preparation only.
