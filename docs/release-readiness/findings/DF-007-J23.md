# DF-007-J23 — Social field and store dispositions

**Status:** locally verified as a policy and physical-schema coverage checkpoint; DF-007 and RR-11 remain open. This does not implement capture.

The [human-readable policy](../SOCIAL-DISPOSITIONS.md) and [machine-readable inventory](../../../apps/social/src/app/social-account-export-dispositions.json) account for all 33 Social entities, 314 mapped columns, 27 relations and the two-column Post/Link junction. Four declared stores cover migration bookkeeping, volatile notification debounce state, mixed process output and operator configuration inputs. Every field/relation group declares classification, action, reason and checked-in source evidence.

## Decisions and findings

Account and profile UUIDs are separate domains. PostShare and ContentReport select profile actors; Presence selects an account and ProfileView selects the viewer account. Paired records require account and current-profile conjunction. Shared community/business administration is not individual ownership of all shared content. Audit receipts use separate actor and subject/dependent branches without exposing unmatched identities or private context.

Legacy attribution stays unverified throughout. Current ownership is selection authority, not proof of historical authorship or consent. Foreign identifiers remain opaque and never trigger recursive content fetches. J21 notification templates remain authoritative; sender/resource/action fields are excluded. Raw invitation email/token, storage paths, component JSON, poll vote arrays, private audit payloads and saved counterpart titles are excluded or require an explicit typed projection. Own contributions remain eligible under the declared selector. A typed projection does not authorize raw passthrough.

The Post/Poll relationship still has no proven persisted owner association. It is explicitly excluded rather than inferred; author-selected Poll definitions remain separately eligible. This is distinct from J22's now-migrated Post/Link relation.
