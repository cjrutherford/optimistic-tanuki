## Verified product facts to carry into decisions

- The HAI public-good philosophy is normative. It calls for user agency, privacy language that matches enforcement, honest marketing, useful error communication, transparent/proportionate safety, and data portability as an architectural goal. Portability is not an existing product promise. Per the settled product direction, locality visibility and core access must not depend on donations; no paid-tier, featured-placement, analytics, or guaranteed-moderation claim may be advertised until the capability is verified.
- RR-11 source and public-root review found no privacy, terms/community-use, support, public abuse contact, account deletion, data export, or status/outage route linked from either product's public entry surfaces.
- OT has authenticated in-product block, mute, and content-report controls. TS exposes classified/seller reports through its privacy endpoint; the current UI says reports go to human review, while a staffed queue/response process is not verified. The gateway rejects message reports pending a separate access check. These are bounded in-product safety flows, not a public abuse/copyright contact route or evidence of general support/privacy-request handling (`apps/client-interface/src/app/components/settings/privacy-settings.component.ts`, `apps/local-hub/src/app/pages/city/city.component.ts`, `apps/gateway/src/controllers/social/privacy/privacy.controller.ts`, `apps/social/src/app/services/privacy.service.ts`). The repository's bug-report tooling is a distinct engineering-report path; it does not establish a privacy request, account lifecycle, abuse or copyright process, or a public support commitment.
- Existing email transport is configured through shared environment settings rather than a per-app public contact destination. Authentication and gateway are separate consumers; registry sender metadata does not establish an inbox for support or privacy requests. The shared email gate remains open; actual invitation receipt is not proven by source configuration (`docs/guides/authentication-setup.md`, `apps/gateway/src/app/app.module.ts`). Bug-report setup documentation states its owner email can be unset and delivery or issue creation can be skipped (`docs/guides/bug-report-setup.md`). Do not publish a route until an operator confirms its real destination, monitoring, delivery and response ownership.
- RR11-I01 accepted the bounded fail-closed gateway contract: a target owner in the current app scope receives 501 because the profile service has no delete handler; other owners and unsupported/global scopes receive 403. No successful profile deletion or dependent-data lifecycle is implemented or proven. No account deletion/export workflow was found. Profile deletion is not account erasure. DF-007 owns the larger cross-service account-data inventory and implementation. Do not claim a manual fallback unless an operator supplies and validates its runbook and record-handling/retention rules.
- The gateway declares an HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax session cookie with a one-hour maximum age; `Secure` defaults on in production but can be overridden (`apps/gateway/src/controllers/authentication/authentication.controller.ts`). OT serializes profile-list and selected-profile objects to local storage, while session credentials are removed from the legacy token key (`apps/client-interface/src/app/state/auth-state.service.ts`). TS's auth-state service restores a cookie session and has no local/session-storage calls in that file (`apps/local-hub/src/app/services/auth-state.service.ts`); this is not a whole-app storage inventory. Both browser entry points start RUM (`apps/client-interface/src/main.ts`, `apps/local-hub/src/main.ts`). Its payload contains app ID, query-stripped pathname, render mode, and metric name/value/timestamp; numeric and UUID path segments and segments over 80 characters are normalized, while other segments remain (`libs/common-ui/src/lib/common-ui/performance-monitor.ts`). The gateway aggregates in in-memory maps; this does not establish infrastructure/log retention outside the service (`apps/gateway/src/performance/performance-telemetry.service.ts`). Authenticated cookie settings, complete production configuration, retention outside process memory, and authenticated/private route behavior have not been verified. Google Fonts was observed on both roots, cdnjs on OT, and Picsum on TS. This was a bounded navigation review, not a complete network inventory.
- RR-01 withdrew unsupported production claims about OT privacy/realtime/permanence and TS safety, funding/donations, featured placement/analytics and paid tiers. No free-forever pricing promise was observed; OT's “Yours Forever” was a permanence claim. RR-01 owns copy dispositions and RR-12 owns production-bundle reconciliation. Do not republish withdrawn capabilities through RR11 policy work.
- HAI is the project-family name used by normative product documents. No reviewed material verifies that it is the operator's registered legal entity or supplies jurisdiction, mailing address, or a legally approved contracting party.

## RR11-I03 — Bounded public-surface architecture plan

**Precondition:** I02 decision record is approved and each linked contact/request route is operationally verified. The user has clarified that the policies and processes do not currently exist. I03 publication and implementation are paused for manual approval; do not publish placeholder policy pages or pretend-to-work forms.

**Consumer applications:** `client-interface` (OT) and `local-hub` (TS). Their Angular route tables are separate. OT's wildcard route redirects to `/feed`; TS's wildcard redirects to `/`, so every public information route must be explicit and tested unauthenticated. The OT landing page currently has a simple footer; neither app shell has a shared policy footer.

**Proposed implementation:** add one small shared public-information UI library only after I02 approval. It should provide an accessible policy-page renderer, shared route/link model, and a product-configured footer/nav link group. Keep approved content as reviewed static per-product data/configuration; do not fetch policy copy from the app registry, user settings, or environment variables. Allow shared text only where the owner explicitly approves identical scope; keep OT/TS feature disclosures and external-resource lists separate where their behavior differs. Product components should supply title, effective date, section headings, content and link destinations; shared UI must not synthesize legal language or default to permissive copy. Reuse current design tokens/common UI primitives rather than introducing app-specific visual systems.

**Routes and visibility:** explicit public routes for only approved content (expected policy/community-use, privacy, and contact/help destinations) in both route tables. Add footer links to both public landing surfaces and the authenticated application shell so they remain available after login. Provide distinct support, privacy request, abuse/copyright and status destinations only when I02 identifies supported channels. If a process is unavailable, do not render a form or mailto link that implies it works. No account deletion/export action is added by I03 unless I02 and DF-007 establish a separately bounded, supported flow.

**Disclosure content:** privacy page should describe the gateway-declared session-cookie behavior, OT's serialized profile-list/selected-profile local-storage use, TS auth-state's cookie restore (while limiting claims to that reviewed file), observed third-party resources by product, and the RUM payload fields and path normalization. It must distinguish verified source fields and browser observations from unresolved production configuration and retention outside the service process. Do not claim an exhaustive inventory, no tracking, or noncollection on authenticated routes until verified. RR-01 owns marketing-copy retraction/rewording and production-bundle reconciliation; I03 must not become an alternate copy fix.

**Verification for a later implementation slice:** direct-load and refresh each approved route while signed out and signed in; confirm the fallback route does not swallow policy paths; verify all links land on approved, reachable destinations; test keyboard navigation, headings, focus, mobile reflow, and accessibility; compare displayed text against the signed-off decision record; ensure OT and TS show their own accurate product disclosures; confirm no new external resources or telemetry fields are introduced; and verify production bundle identity in RR-01/RR-12. No route/form, legal content, or production behavior is accepted by this plan.
