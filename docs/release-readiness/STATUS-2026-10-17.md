## What is actually missing before public release

1. **Implement account deletion and portable export.** DF-007 is a mandatory release prerequisite, even though it lives in the deferred register. It needs cross-service data mapping, deletion and portable JSON export including media, plus provider/backup validation.

2. **Finish the remaining privacy and session behavior.** Seven-day rolling inactivity expiry is recorded as not implemented. Complete the tracking inventory and prove age/consent controls; implement and verify the 90-day deletion requirement for all account-activity logs, including provider and backup copies. Existing guardian-record cleanup does not satisfy this broader requirement.

3. **Establish real operational and public-policy support.** The contact page and approved guardian notice are not a complete public policy or operating process. Finish the approved privacy/terms/age disclosures, inbox delivery and triage evidence, privacy/abuse/copyright handling and retention enforcement. Do not add an unapproved response-time promise.

4. **Close real identity and invitation delivery.** Exercise the providers actually presented to users: OAuth callbacks, registration/email verification, recovery/reset and invitation receipt, expiry/replay/cancel and failure visibility. Existing registration or guardian email evidence does not automatically prove community invitations.

5. **Finish integrated accessibility and reliability acceptance.** Run the remaining OT/TS critical journeys, manual assistive-technology review, full Towne Square acceptance and thresholded deployed media/load/resource qualification. Passing ordinary E2E tests does not replace these criteria.

6. **Qualify the reviewed deployment, then finish launch materials.** After the implementation and independent gates are closed, correlate the reviewed source to deployed images and served bundles, rerun the release matrix, reconcile public claims, obtain the operator’s release decision and finish onboarding/screenshots/checklist.

This is a recommended completion order for the remaining work; the master audit and each slice’s acceptance criteria remain authoritative.
