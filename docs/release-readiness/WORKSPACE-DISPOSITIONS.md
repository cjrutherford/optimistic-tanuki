# Workspace account export dispositions

O01-P is the internal policy and physical-catalog check for one bounded part of O01. O01-P, O01-C, and O01-I are internal checks within that single O01 delivery; passing P does not mean O01 or account export delivery is complete.

The [version 1 inventory](../../apps/workspace/src/app/workspace-account-export-dispositions.json) accounts for all 12 persisted Workspace columns and is checked against the entity metadata returned by `loadDatabase` plus an optional physical PostgreSQL catalog. It requires exactly one local entity (`Workspace`), no relations or association tables, and the local `migrations` bookkeeping table. Three local stores are declared: TypeORM migrations, `Workspace.processOutput`, and `Workspace.configurationInputs`. The latter two are excluded; external log sinks and retention remain unknown, and configuration inputs may contain operator secrets. Cross-service Permissions, Social, and AppConfigurator references are not local registered stores.

The approved output is a typed stewardship receipt containing all 12 registered fields. Select rows only when both the authenticated account ID and a currently owned profile ID match the recorded `ownerUserId` and `ownerProfileId`; selection is account-wide across `appScope`. Include both principal IDs in the receipt as personal identifiers and selection keys; their presence does not establish exclusive ownership. The authenticated `appId` authenticates the request and never narrows row selection. Recorded ownership, scope, and attribution are not proof of historical authority or consent. Treat `slug`, `displayName`, `appScope`, and the `sourceService` enum as inert shared registration labels/context, not authorship or authority claims. Preserve `createdAt` and `updatedAt` as ISO strings without loss or truncation. Include `sourceId` only as an opaque reference alongside `sourceService`; never dereference it or fetch source content.

Do not traverse members, roles, counterpart records, or source content. O01-P proves policy and catalog parity only; it does not implement capture, verify authorization against live identities, or establish delivery completion.

The opt-in PostgreSQL specification is `workspace-dispositions.postgres.spec.ts`. It requires the private fixture marker and matching `POSTGRES_HOST`, `POSTGRES_PORT`, and `POSTGRES_DB` overrides, runs read-only without migrations or synchronization, checks the two expected tables and 15 columns, verifies both app-scope unique indexes, and compares row counts before and after. Its catalog evidence is written to `WORKSPACE_DISPOSITIONS_RUNTIME_ROOT/catalog-readback.json`.

O01 capture and authenticated leased integration are now locally verified in the [delivery finding](./findings/DF-007-O01.md). The policy itself remains the P check; delivery completion requires the C/I evidence recorded there.
