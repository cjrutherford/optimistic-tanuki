**Goal:** Integrate every identified business-directory POC service and user workflow into Town Square/Local Hub, using shared platform services and UI, with automatic directory availability and acquisition orchestration for every eligible town already in the database and every town subsequently created.

### Provider decision and publication policy

**Recommendation: Overture-first for durable bulk acquisition; do not replace the primary source merely because this POC currently has private-only approval.** This is a proposed product/source decision, conditional on the gates below. Its basis is the existing pinned extraction, streaming/checkpoint handlers, field-evidence pipeline and v3 taxonomy work; global bulk distribution fits automatic town onboarding and amortizes scans across towns. Current official [Places guidance](https://docs.overturemaps.org/guides/places/) describes global coverage and permissive source licensing, while documenting duplicates, junk and incomplete properties. It supports feasibility, not a promise of complete or accurate business coverage. The [attribution page](https://docs.overturemaps.org/attribution/) maps contributors to licenses/notices, including CDLA Permissive 2.0, Apache 2.0 and AllThePlaces CC0. Current guidance states Places contains no OSM data; do not generalize licenses or ODbL obligations across Places, other Overture themes, basemaps and third-party enrichment. These current pages are **not release-specific clearance** for every field of `2026-09-23.0`.

Practical provider choices:

Reuse existing local artifacts privately for replay and, only after explicit clearance, eligible public projections. Choose an exact release in S4a: retain September if its evidence can be established, otherwise acquire a newly pinned release with its own policy. New acquisition does not automatically fix rights or quality, and publication of the old cohort need not block safe use of an independently cleared newer cohort. Costs include storage, scan compute and refresh operations even without per-query API credits.
- **Geoapify Places:** optional bounded gap-filling/comparison source, or an explicit fallback if Overture fails the publication/quality gate and a pilot proves suitable coverage and costs. Its current [Places FAQ](https://www.geoapify.com/places-api/) permits storage and redistribution with attribution and identifies OSM as its principal source; its [terms](https://www.geoapify.com/terms-and-conditions/) require OSM attribution, Geoapify attribution on the free plan, and API-specific requirements. The POC's 24-hour/non-exportable fixture/private-dev policy is conservative configuration, not proof that the provider universally prohibits durable storage/export. Keep those restrictions until a reviewed replacement policy covers the actual API, account/plan, acquisition date, dataset rights (including applicable ODbL duties), fields and use. Assess pagination/request credits and deployment-wide budgets before making it primary. Do not use public page-load calls as the acquisition strategy.
- **Geoapify reverse locality/address enrichment:** distinct from Places. Source `tools/overture/geoapify-reverse-locality.ts` declares indefinite attributed retention but emits non-exportable approximate nearby-city evidence, while the base Geoapify policy differs. Reconcile these policies and the reverse-geocoding terms independently; do not apply the Places FAQ as blanket permission. Owner-managed business pages remain a separate first-party workflow with verified authority, not a shortcut for clearing imported fields.

**Source-wide policy versus per-record review:** approve reusable policy at provider/product + exact release or documented compatible release range + contributor/license + field + intended operation (store, public display, export) scope. A designated source-use reviewer records dated evidence, fingerprints/copies of applicable terms/notices, permitted geography, retention and policy version. Machine evaluation then applies that policy to every town and record. It is not necessary to manually approve each town or every well-formed record. A sample review validates the rules and quality thresholds; it cannot waive failures outside the sample. Unknown contributors/releases, malformed field source pointers, conflicting licenses or unreviewed transforms fail closed into exception queues. Required-field failures quarantine the listing; optional-field failures omit only that field when a usable lawful listing remains. Human rights/taxonomy/merge review handles exceptions and appeals with audit/undo; an operator click cannot manufacture missing upstream rights.

**Required rights-decision artifact.** For each provider product and applicable source cohort/release (or documented compatible range), persist a versioned, machine-consumable policy plus a human-readable evidence record. At minimum capture source/release and artifact fingerprints; contributor and upstream-license identifiers; exact field and derived-field lineage; intended operation (`ingest`, `retain`, `display`, `export`, `enrich`); allow/deny/unknown outcome and rationale; geographic and use limits; attribution/notices and their applicability; retention and withdrawal requirements; date and immutable copy/fingerprint of reviewed terms; responsible reviewer; decision date; policy version; expiry/re-review trigger; and links to sampled negative tests. For mixed records, evaluate each field's complete contributing chain, not merely the final listing's nominal provider. Store reviewer approval separately from deploy-time enablement. Unknown or conflicting evidence fails closed. Include a small representative example set of **allow, deny and unknown** decisions with the exact evaluator output, plus a test proving that policy withdrawal removes already indexed/cached public fields. A human click cannot substitute for upstream rights.

### Recommended architecture and alternatives

Recommend a platform-owned directory bounded context: retain Social ownership of communities; own directory data, spatial queries and acquisition state in a dedicated directory service/database schema, and use a worker for queues and provider I/O. Route public reads and authenticated operator actions through the existing gateway. Prefer compatible shared PostgreSQL/Redis infrastructure with separate ownership/namespaces over deploying a duplicate platform stack; verify PostGIS, queue isolation and resource capacity first. New service/project names and files are **proposed**, to be resolved in Slice 0 rather than treated as existing paths.

Keep POC library responsibilities when useful, under collision-safe workspace names. Integrate capabilities rather than preserving the standalone POC shell. Reuse authentication/permissions from the platform; imported listener separation is an additional boundary, not a substitute for authorization.

Alternatives considered:

- Embed all acquisition, queues and spatial persistence into Social: fewer deployables but couples community availability and migrations to provider-heavy jobs. Use only if Slice 0 proves existing service ownership and operational isolation are adequate.
- Deploy the POC unchanged and iframe/link it: fastest demonstration but retains fixed geography, duplicate auth/UI/configuration and manual run setup; does not satisfy integration or all-town automation.
- Run one POC deployment per town: rejected; creates per-town infrastructure, secrets and setup.
- Query paid live providers on each public page load: rejected as the default; unpredictable cost, latency and data rights. Read durable policy-filtered projections and acquire asynchronously.

Human approval of the ownership/identity ADR is required before implementation. Licensing, service placement and eligibility decisions remain explicit gates, not implied by this recommendation.

## 2. Product and automation contract

Follow `docs/product/HAI-PUBLIC-GOOD-PHILOSOPHY.md`: directory utility must not depend on payment, GPS access or manipulative ranking. Membership and residence are not inferred from coordinates. Keep sponsorship separate and visibly labeled; no paid eligibility or undisclosed ranking boost. Public claims must describe demonstrated coverage, with timestamps and limitations.

**Automatic availability** means every eligible DB town resolves to a directory view/API and a durable provisioning/acquisition status without an operator clicking “run,” editing seeds, making a region, adding credentials or configuring a deployment for that town. **Populated data** means approved, licensed observations were actually acquired and projected. Availability does not guarantee provider coverage, business existence or exhaustive data.

Slice 0 must define eligibility across city/town, county/region/neighborhood, `appScope`, system/non-system and private communities. Minimum requirement is all public Town Square city/town locality communities already in DB plus future ones; broader locality types require explicit rules. Never silently omit a town because coordinates, slug or a briefing association are missing. Record automatic resolution work or a visible `needs_geometry`/invalid-context state; report those exceptions in the denominator. Private communities must not become publicly discoverable through this integration.

Recommended automatic lifecycle:

1. Reconcile a paginated authoritative DB snapshot of eligible communities, including non-seed records; use Community UUID, not mutable display name/slug, as stable external identity.
2. Publish durable locality create/update/delete/scope-change events from the owning transaction where feasible, with an outbox. Consume idempotently. A scheduled reconciliation repairs missed events, seed/import writes and downtime; it is required even if events exist.
3. Upsert versioned directory context/geometry mapping and enqueue bounded acquisition by locality, geometry version, provider release, category policy and query-plan version. No hardcoded seat allowlist and no per-town operator approval in the normal path.
4. Derive geometry through one global policy and authoritative licensed boundary source; permit validated fallback radius only with explicit labeling. Resolve missing geometry automatically where possible; ambiguous or unavailable boundaries remain visible exceptions. Do not generate a four-seat Voronoi partition for the whole DB by accident. Define border inclusion/tie rules, overlapping areas and cross-town business associations.
5. Schedule backfill and refresh fairly under deployment-wide provider budgets. Use shared release scans/partition caches where possible, rather than downloading a global dataset per town. Durable cursors/checkpoints, retry/backoff, cancellation/resume and idempotent writes survive restarts.
6. Project only policy-eligible records to public search/detail/map/export. New towns are visible before acquisition completes. Last good public data may remain during a failure only while retention/rights still permit it, with a freshness label.
7. Town rename/geometry change reindexes/reacquires without duplicating businesses. Scope/privacy changes or deletion withdraw public associations promptly and cancel obsolete work; they do not delete unrelated towns' shared business evidence.

Define separate state dimensions for provisioning, acquisition and publication. At minimum distinguish queued/running, successful zero results, partial/stale data, policy-blocked/unconfigured provider, invalid geometry, retryable infrastructure failure and permanent acquisition failure. A `200 []` alone cannot explain these states. API errors must preserve correlation IDs and retry semantics without leaking credentials or operator details.

## 4. Review protocol and bounded slices

Execute sequentially unless a dependency explicitly allows otherwise. S4a.1/S4a.2 establish the policy and private pilot; S5/S6 and the essential S7 policy-withdrawal/correction controls then supply the public surface before S4a.3 canary approval. Each slice delivers one review packet: scoped diff, touched Nx apps/libs, contract/schema/API changes, screenshots or operator transcript where relevant, exact commands and results, fixture/live distinction, checks not run, known limitations, rollback and remaining capability ledger entries. Record reviewer decision (`approved`, `changes requested`, `deferred`) before advancing. Source code and unit tests alone do not satisfy acceptance.

Candidate report paths below are proposed documents under `docs/plans/town-square-business-directory/`. Keep the present planning task to this file; create packets during later authorized execution. No slice may expand silently: split it and obtain review if its migration, provider or workflow scope exceeds one reviewable change.
