# S4a.2 synthetic preparation evidence

**Status: partial; not an accepted private data pilot.** This work uses fabricated in-memory records and policies. It performs no provider calls, reads no POC records, and does not implement or enable a Directory endpoint, database, index, cache or export.

The [prototype](../../../scripts/s4a2-private-pilot.mjs) explores operation- and field-lineage checks, a restricted candidate allowlist, explicit sensitivity and adult classifications, attribution on allowed reads, and withdrawal of its in-memory result. A missing or unknown required lineage blocks the fixture; an optional field lacking an allowed lineage is omitted. The synthetic policy and lineage are supplied by the test and are not evidence that a real contributor chain is complete, any rights exist, or any data is suitable for publication. This prototype is not connected to the S4a.1 draft policy or usable as a production authorizer.

On 2026-11-06, `NX_DAEMON=false NX_ISOLATE_PLUGINS=false pnpm nx run directory:test-s4a2-private-pilot --skip-nx-cache` passed six synthetic tests. They exercise unknown and mixed lineage, operation denial, optional omission, scalar-only candidate fields, sensitive/residential/adult classification, attribution and in-memory withdrawal. The test surface names are functions over the same object; they do not prove real direct-ID, map, search, cache or export enforcement, or persistent invalidation after a policy withdrawal.

The [quality protocol](S4a2-quality-protocol.md) proposes pre-sample thresholds, stratification and uncertainty handling. Its numbers are not yet approved or frozen, and no acceptance sample has been drawn or evaluated. S4a.1 remains unapproved: the user volunteered to review it but has not granted source-use permission. Subsequently implement a durable rights-enforcing data path and real surface/index/cache withdrawal tests, freeze the approved sampling protocol, execute the private quality study, and obtain its review. Public rollout and export require their separate gates.

## 2026-11-08 policy-direction tests

Following the user's choices, the synthetic evaluator can include an optional website, business phone or street address only when each has an explicit business-verification flag, source/operation rights and the required notices. Unknown notice requirements or omitted required notices deny the affected source mapping. URLs must be public HTTP(S) hostnames rather than credentials or literal private addresses. The evaluator models adult display as a visitor opt-in on each read and denies adult shared-cache reads. Sensitive and residential classifications remain fail-closed. `directory:test-s4a2-private-pilot` passed 10/10 synthetic tests, including explicit source-notice requirements and rejection of inherited rights and visitor flags.

These flags are caller assertions, not independently authenticated verification; this evaluator remains in-memory and is not the Directory runtime, an authorization service, a public API, or proof of safe cache/withdrawal behavior. The existing private extractor and sink still use their narrower projection. No provider records were acquired and no approval to launch or acquire is inferred from these tests.

A separate synthetic sampler accepts a caller-supplied frozen frame of hashed record identities, explicit town/contributor allocations, a 32-byte seed and release/protocol fingerprints. It deterministically ranks records with HMAC-SHA-256, records frame/plan digests and nominal per-stratum selection ratios, and reports unrepresented strata or an empty frame as incomplete. Four focused synthetic tests passed. This is a planning primitive only: it does not establish that a seed was chosen independently, that inputs were preregistered, that any particular allocation or confidence method was approved, or that quality thresholds passed. It has not selected or inspected provider records.

### Full-frame review completeness foundation

Following the user's decision that two reviewers assess every eligible record, `scripts/s4a2-review-coverage.mjs` provides a synthetic, in-memory completeness check: it detects missing assessments, unresolved or unknown outcomes, duplicate or out-of-frame reviews, and unsupported disagreement resolutions. Its focused Nx target passed on 2026-11-08. It reports coverage only, not a quality pass, and cannot establish reviewer identity, independence, evidence quality, complete frame construction or durable audit. No real review or source data was used.

The coverage check also distinguishes an empty merge frame (`not-applicable`, with no validation claim) from an empty listing frame (`inconclusive`). The focused Nx test passed for both cases. This synthetic check does not establish that the frame is complete or that a merge system is accurate.

### Public projection foundation after revised objective

`projectPublicOverture` applies an explicit `publicDisplay` grant in addition to ingestion and retention grants, rejects uncertain, residential or sensitive classifications, and requires visitor opt-in for adult listings. It returns attribution, required notices and terms references with the minimal listing. The focused synthetic Nx test and Directory build passed. This function is not connected to a serving endpoint or persisted public projection; its caller-supplied policy and classification need trusted authorities. Its optional phone, website and address projection requires explicit caller-supplied business verification and individual public-display rights; additional required notices are returned. The focused synthetic tests pass, but this does not establish trusted verification, a serving endpoint or a usable local directory.
