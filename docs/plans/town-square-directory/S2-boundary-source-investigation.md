# S2 boundary source investigation — 2026-11-07

The user requested verified boundaries where available and a clearly labeled fallback where needed. This is an investigation, not an imported boundary, a source-use determination for every jurisdiction, or proof of S2 acceptance.

The US Census Bureau's [2026 TIGER/Line release](https://www.census.gov/geographies/mapping-files/2026/geo/tiger-line-file.html) reports legal boundary names and shapes as of January 1, 2026. It is a candidate source for US incorporated places, with an explicit vintage rather than a claim of current legal boundaries. Its [geography definitions](https://tigerweb.geo.census.gov/tigerwebmain/TIGERweb_geography_details.html) distinguish incorporated places from census-designated places, and explain that certain towns in New England, New York and Wisconsin are treated as minor civil divisions rather than incorporated places. Therefore a place-only lookup cannot cover every US town, and a census-designated place must not be presented as an incorporated municipal boundary.

A source-verified Census polygon can therefore be labeled with its source, vintage and statistical purpose; it must not be described as a guaranteed current legal boundary. ## Private pinned boundary reader

The target now has `scripts/census-boundary-reader.mjs`, a private preparation primitive informed by the POC's fingerprinted input and bounded-archive approach. It accepts an exact state, layer and GEOID, a caller-supplied archive size/hash and reference point; makes a private hash-checked snapshot; checks ZIP member metadata and the expected Census NAD83 projection; transforms the polygon to WGS84; and rejects a missing or duplicate identifier, invalid/empty geometry, mismatched state or an outside reference point. It returns `candidate`, explicitly reports source authority and community identity as unverified, and neither writes to a database nor serves a route. The source reference, fingerprint, vintage and Census attribution accompany the result. Supplied hashes, identifiers and points are caller assertions; this function does not authenticate the archive, verify Social identity or grant rights. It must not be exposed to arbitrary untrusted ZIP uploads without further archive-parser resource-hardening review.
