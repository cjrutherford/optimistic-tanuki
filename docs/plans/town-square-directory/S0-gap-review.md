## What can move to S1

After human review of the S0 decisions and explicit authorization of the next slice, S1 can implement only the bounded platform foundation: selected API/worker packaging, directory-owned persistence and generated migrations, health/readiness, approved PostgreSQL/PostGIS/Redis use, queue isolation, configuration, gateway ingress and role enforcement. S1 may use sanitized synthetic fixtures and may prove deployment topology without live provider calls, raw source data, public publication, or paid operations. It must preserve the unresolved policy items as fail-closed gates, not silently decide them in code.

The following should remain later gates: town eligibility/backfill (S2/S3); provider rights, acquisition and quality pilot (S4); public API/UI and publication (S5/S6); operator review and correction workflows (S7); export (S8); production operations and recovery (S9); integrated acceptance (S10). No slice is approved by this research note.
