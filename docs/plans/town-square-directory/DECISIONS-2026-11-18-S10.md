# S10 owner decisions (2026-11-18)

The owner made these decisions directly when S10 implementation started. They replace the matching "Decisions for the owner" items in the [S10 acceptance scorecard](S10-acceptance-scorecard.md).

| #   | Topic                   | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| --- | ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| D1  | Acquisition             | **Overture, automatic.** Acquisition is authorized for Overture only. A worker acquires each eligible town from the pinned Overture release under the rights policy, the budget ledger and the kill switch. New towns fill without an operator. Every other provider stays unauthorized. |
| D2  | Geometry                | **Automatic from Census TIGER.** Each town is matched to its TIGER place (or county subdivision) automatically. A single unambiguous match is installed as the town's geometry. Ambiguous or missing matches go to the existing boundary review queue.                                   |
| D3  | Standalone AllThePlaces | **Implement.** AllThePlaces gets its own provider wiring under CC0, separate from the copy carried inside Overture.                                                                                                                                                                      |
| D4  | Geoapify                | **Implement as private enrichment only.** It stays denied for public display and export.                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| D5  | Remaining deferrals     | `/regions` and the fixture/sample provider modes stay deferred in [DF-008](../../release-readiness/DEFERRED-FEATURES.md).                                                                                                                                                                |
| D6  | Shared-UI regression    | **Bring up the full stack.** Start the missing services (forum, chat, OAuth), register business-site, and rerun client-interface, forgeofwill and business-site until they are clean or the remaining failures are real regressions.                                                     |

## Still open

These were not decided in this round and are not assumed:

- the three unknown ledger rows (normalize stage, dedupe uncertainty, OpenAPI regeneration);
- the S7 address auto-approval decisions ([§12](S7-address-review-auto-approval.md));
- provenance for the 4,850 pilot rows;
- resuming publication, or a first-public-release canary;
- Geoapify credentials and budget ceiling, and the AllThePlaces archive source;
- acquisition operating ceilings and SLOs beyond the existing budget ledger defaults.

Until they are decided, publication stays paused, Geoapify runs only when a key is configured, and acquisition uses the existing budget ledger ceilings.
