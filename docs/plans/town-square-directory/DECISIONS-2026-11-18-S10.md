# S10 owner decisions (2026-11-18)

The owner made these decisions directly when S10 implementation started. They replace the matching "Decisions for the owner" items in the [S10 acceptance scorecard](S10-acceptance-scorecard.md).

| #   | Topic                   | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| --- | ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| D1  | Acquisition             | **Overture, automatic.** Acquisition is authorized for Overture only. A worker acquires each eligible town from the pinned Overture release under the rights policy, the budget ledger and the kill switch. New towns fill without an operator. Every other provider stays unauthorized. |
| D2  | Geometry                | **Automatic from Census TIGER.** Each town is matched to its TIGER place (or county subdivision) automatically. A single unambiguous match is installed as the town's geometry. Ambiguous or missing matches go to the existing boundary review queue.                                   |
| D3  | Standalone AllThePlaces | **Implement.** AllThePlaces gets its own provider wiring under CC0, separate from the copy carried inside Overture.                                                                                                                                                                      |
| D4  | Geoapify                | **Implement as private enrichment only.** It stays denied for public display and export.                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| D5  | Remaining deferrals     | `/regions` and the fixture/sample provider modes stay deferred in [DF-008](../../release-readiness/DEFERRED-FEATURES.md).                                                                                                                                                                |
| D6  | Shared-UI regression    | **Bring up the full stack.** Start the missing services (forum, chat, OAuth), register business-site, and rerun client-interface, forgeofwill and business-site until they are clean or the remaining failures are real regressions.                                                     |

## Third round (2026-11-18, operating targets)

These complete the targets the [2026-11-07 decisions](DECISIONS-2026-11-07.md) left open. Daily reconciliation, 99% of changes queued within 24 hours, a 90-day refresh, the stale label at 120 days, withdrawal within 24 hours and the quality limits were already set there.

| #   | Topic             | Decision                                                                                                                                                                              |
| --- | ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| D15 | Fill time         | **7 days.** A new or changed town reaches filled, zero, blocked or failed within 7 days.                                                                                              |
| D16 | Retention         | **Until replaced.** Records stay until a newer scan replaces them or a withdrawal removes them. There is no age-based deletion. A shorter source rule still wins (Geoapify: 30 days). |
| D17 | Corrections       | **Hide now, triage in 3 days.** A safety or privacy report hides the listing at once until it is reviewed. Every report is acknowledged and triaged within 3 days.                    |
| D18 | Merge-error check | **Random sample.** The 2% wrong-merge limit is measured on a fixed random sample of pairs for each review round, not on every merge.                                                  |

The owner also asked for: scheduled refresh and refill after a boundary change; an Owner Console screen for duplicate review; a manual "run this town now" action; and real borders for the towns with no TIGER match, locked so the automatic matcher does not replace them. The owner will handle the outside monitor and the production items personally.

## Outcomes on the local stack

- D7: 236 towns matched automatically. 19 more were installed from Census and locked against the matcher (`7ab42a00e`), making 255. The other 8 were decided in D19 to D26 and applied live 2026-11-18: 5 merged into neighbouring towns, Ponte Vedra Beach renamed to Palm Valley, Corolla and Palm Valley locked, Edisto Beach added, the blank row retired (`c5cfa49c7`, `6928657b9`, `341971cde`, `0788c6930`). All 259 eligible towns have a ready boundary. The Social seed re-saves every community, so a seed run makes the directory re-match every town's border once; it recovered within minutes.
- D8: Geoapify is live. D30 replaced its monthly cent ledger with a daily credit ledger of 3,000 credits a day (UTC reset); 1 credit per Place Details request is assumed, not documented by Geoapify.

- D11: deployed, but no refresh has run since, so nothing has been auto-approved yet.
- D13: the worker queued more than 6,500 duplicate cases. Part of that volume comes from towns that serve old and fresh scans until the swap. Review works through the gateway route and the Owner Console duplicate screen (`1888dd1dd`).

- D15 to D18: built (`6a386f329`, `1888dd1dd`, `5b95a932e`): 90-day refresh, the 7-day fill-time metric, a manual run-now action, hide-on-report with a 3-day triage metric, and the random duplicate sample.
