## Proposed source

As checked on 2026-11-06, Overture lists **2026-10-21.0**, schema **v2.0.0**, as its current release. The [official release calendar](https://docs.overturemaps.org/release-calendar/) schedules the next release for 2026-11-18 and says public release files are retained for at most 60 days. Recheck the current release and its availability before a later acquisition; do not substitute a different release under this proposal. The [release notes](https://docs.overturemaps.org/blog/2026/10/21/release-notes/) identify the release paths and report that Uberall, Birdeye and OpenTravel were added as Places contributors. The [Places guide](https://docs.overturemaps.org/guides/places/) lists contributors and reports known quality problems, including duplicates, junk and incomplete properties.

The proposed input is **only the Places theme** from that release, limited initially to the same four Georgia towns as the earlier proof of concept: Douglas, Tifton, Valdosta and Waycross. The official Places path is `s3://overturemaps-us-west-2/release/2026-10-21.0/theme=places/type=place/`, with an Azure equivalent documented in the guide. The exact eligible boundaries, selection method, object identities and extraction size/cost must be established before acquisition. This document is not an artifact manifest; none has been downloaded or fingerprinted for this proposal.

Re-run and compare immediately before a future acquisition; the envelope is the old POC envelope, not proof of current target-town boundaries.

The extractor therefore does not discard groups on that basis. The nine groups' column chunks total 15,375,391 compressed bytes, but this is not a transfer estimate or cost quote. No business records were read. Recheck the source identity and actual target boundaries before use.

## Proposed private use

1. After a separately documented approved extraction procedure, acquire the smallest practical four-town dataset into an isolated private pilot environment. Record the release, exact objects and fingerprints, schema, extraction method and boundary versions. No assumption is made about actual transfer cost until measured or estimated.
2. Inventory every observed contributor, license, field and transformation. Deny or quarantine records and fields with unknown or conflicting lineage; do not assume contributors newly added to the global release appear in the four-town subset.
3. The current private pilot projection remains limited to business name, location, category and provenance. On 2026-11-08 the user directed that an eventual public listing may also include verified business websites, publicly listed business phone numbers and nonresidential business street addresses, subject to verified field/source rights and required notices; uncertain or conflicting fields stay hidden. A valid URL or a source-provided value alone does not establish that it belongs to a business or is safe to display. The private projection has not yet been expanded, and email, personal/residential information and enrichment remain outside it. Adult and sensitive-location handling and actual acquisition, retention, review and rollout remain separately gated.
4. Review the private pilot with the preregistered full-frame review and quality protocol once its methodology and thresholds are approved. The [draft protocol](S4a2-quality-protocol.md) is not yet approved or frozen. No real acceptance sample has been drawn.

The proposal grants no public display, public API, export, redistribution, provider enablement or production rollout. Those decisions remain separate. A user-selected release does not itself establish rights or privacy compliance.
