## Options to prepare for the user

1. Overture-first private pilot: identify the applicable notices and source combinations for a concrete release and permit only the reviewed private operations. Estimate transfer and cost before acquisition.
2. Add Geoapify only for a clearly identified feature and product after checking its specific data conditions, costs and OSM-derived obligations.
3. Evaluate whether direct AllThePlaces adds enough unique coverage to justify a separate pipeline; otherwise use only its contribution to the selected Overture release where permitted.

These are research options, not recommendations to activate providers. Continue building fixture paths and the restrictions needed to honor the user's eventual decisions. A complete field-by-field decision matrix, cost proposal and reviewer approval remain open.
