# Private directory quality check — proposal for your review

**Not approved or preregistered.** The user chose the four Georgia POC towns for the proposed pilot and directed planning for 400 randomly selected records and two reviewers. The final method, thresholds and sampling remain unapproved. This proposal does not authorize acquiring or sampling real records. The working POC is accepted for local evaluation; this check would answer a different question: how accurate and usable the selected real records are for the directory's intended use. The more detailed draft is [S4a2-quality-protocol.md](S4a2-quality-protocol.md).

1. **Agree on the scope first.** Choose the approved source release, towns, boundary/fallback rules, fields, reviewer roles and lawful review materials. Record what is excluded and why. Resolve source-use conditions before acquisition. Residential and sensitive locations remain blocked pending review; adult records require explicit opt-in.
2. **Fix the method before looking at the acceptance sample.** Propose a reproducible random sample of 400 records across towns and contributors, or the entire eligible set if it is smaller. Add up to 20 randomly selected records from each predeclared sensitive, sparse, possible-duplicate and possible-closure group. Record how records were selected and their chances of selection. If the sample or an important group is too small to judge, report the result as inconclusive.
3. **Review the records.** Propose two reviewers with a third person to settle disagreements; alternatively the user may approve a simpler process and accept its limitations. Compare the permitted information against agreed independent evidence. Keep personal and residential details out of the summary.
4. **Proposed thresholds, not a finding about the POC.** For duplicates, junk and wrong-town/outside-boundary records, propose that the upper 95% confidence bound for each rate be at most 5%; for missing required fields, at most 10%; for incorrect merges of distinct locations, at most 2%. Agree on a separate pair sample and its minimum size for merge assessment. Record a valid stratified, weighted interval method before sampling. An unsupported estimate or insufficient pair sample is inconclusive. Record suspected closures separately; do not assert closure accuracy without a permitted independent basis.
5. **Decision.** Report the numbers checked, excluded and blocked; rates and uncertainty by town/source where meaningful; reviewer disagreements; and any unauthorized fields or sensitive disclosures. The proposal requires zero observed unauthorized disclosures, but this does not prove zero overall risk. If a bound fails or evidence is inconclusive, explain the issue and offer repair, narrower scope or another independently sampled pilot; do not change thresholds after seeing results to manufacture a pass.

**Still to resolve in the final protocol:** exact sampling frame and allocation, reviewer assignment, threshold values, evidence sources and cost, pair-sampling design, and what to do if a threshold fails. The selection of 400 and two reviewers does not approve the remaining method or any targeted oversampling. Public release and export remain separate decisions even if the private quality check passes.

On 2026-11-08 the user selected a full-frame review when fewer than 400 eligible records exist, and independent reviews followed by evidence comparison; unresolved disagreements make the affected result inconclusive. Other method and source-use gates remain open.
