# S2 Census boundary reader: private preparation

Goal: reuse the POC's pinned-input and bounded ZIP ideas to read a specific Census geography as an **unapproved candidate**, without guessing town identity, writing to Directory, or enabling a public route. Existing four-seat Voronoi service areas remain approximate and cannot be reused as official municipal boundaries.

1. Add focused tests using locally generated synthetic Census-shaped archives: exact GEOID and matching reference point; no match, duplicate GEOID, point outside, malformed metadata, oversized archive/members, wrong SHA and unsafe ZIP members must fail closed. Test that output identifies its source and remains a candidate.
2. Build a private reader around the declared DuckDB spatial dependency and bounded ZIP metadata validation. Verify SHA-256 and byte count over a private snapshot, exact vintage/state/layer/GEOID and expected shapefile members. Require and validate Census NAD83 projection metadata; transform to WGS84 explicitly. Validate nonempty polygon geometry and reference-point coverage. The supplied fingerprint and GEOID are assertions, not evidence of a review or authorization.
3. Run the narrow Nx tests, then check the reader against the free official Georgia 2026 Places archive and the four previously corroborated GEOIDs. Do not copy source-community names, owner details or provider business records; delete temporary source files after the test. Report archive identity, validity/coverage and limits.
4. A later step must establish trustworthy Social-to-GEOID bindings, handle overlapping/ambiguous jurisdictions and missing reference points with labeled fallback, and integrate privacy-safe lifecycle and actual routes. A candidate from this reader cannot be served publicly or marked verified merely because its shape covers a point.
