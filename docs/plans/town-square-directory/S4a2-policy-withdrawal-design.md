# S4a.2 policy-cohort withdrawal

**Status: local database foundation implemented and synthetically tested; real-source use and production acceptance remain open.** Policy and scan identities remain caller assertions until an independently authenticated approval authority is connected.

A durable scan-to-policy membership and policy revocation prevent a scan from being rebound or resumed after withdrawal. `withdrawPolicy` first commits the policy revocation under the policy lock. All sink load, completion and write paths check that revocation under the same lock, so a crash or cleanup error after revocation leaves the cohort denied. The method then deletes up to 500 retained rows or per-row revocation markers per transaction, with at most 64 batches and a five-second scheduling window per call. It returns `{complete:false}` when another call is required; callers must keep retrying and monitor progress. Completion means no retained rows, row markers, checkpoint or completion markers remain for the policy's member scans. Policy revocation, immutable scan membership and scan locks are retained to fence resurrection. Unrelated policies are unaffected.

A fresh disposable PostGIS test exercises registration/withdrawal ordering, injected cleanup failure after durable revocation, unrelated policies, a 1,200-row interrupted cleanup and resume, and denial of later loads. The initial one-transaction all-or-nothing implementation has been superseded. A process failure before the first revocation transaction commits still requires the operator to retry; there is no separate durable request queue or alert. The loop's five-second scheduling window does not bound the duration of an individual transaction beyond its database timeouts, and production-scale operation has not been measured.

No public runtime or index/cache reads are connected to this private sink. Before real source data, require an independently authenticated policy authority and operator action, a durable withdrawal request/monitoring path, complete coverage of every storage and display surface, production-scale timing, and a recovery/rollback method that preserves policy revocations and membership. The generated downgrade drops policy/membership tables and cannot be used as a rights-preserving rollback. No provider acquisition or public use is authorized by these tests.
