# OT and TS landing copy refresh

Keep the existing layouts, imagery, themes and navigation. Refresh the language
and page metadata so readers can distinguish the two applications and understand
their current workflows without reading implementation or release notes.

OT is centered on people, relationships and interests. Describe profiles,
following, posts, the feed, separate discovery, optional communities and
conversation. Communities are one way to participate, not a prerequisite for
having a social network.

TS is centered on place. Describe browsing available localities, opening public
community pages, joining communities, participating in chat and exploring or
posting classifieds. Listings and messaging do not establish that payments or
completed sales are available. Do not imply that every place is covered or that
test fixtures represent adoption.

State early-access context clearly, then describe supported actions directly.
Remove repetitive claims that locally verified workflows are unavailable or
merely planned. Preserve the limits on public-release qualification and avoid
guarantees about delivery, encryption, permanence, payments or complete removal
of external copies. Account-data language must match request status and coverage
rather than promise instant erasure.

Review the new copy against current routes and local workflow evidence. Keep
historical R6 receipts and claim snapshots unchanged; add a dated current copy
review instead. Verify fresh rendered pages, metadata and CTA destinations at
desktop and mobile widths, with screenshots tied to the current source.
