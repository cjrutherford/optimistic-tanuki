## Tifton Council denies downtown entertainment district plan

The Tifton City Council decided not to approve a new ordinance for a downtown entertainment district. Council members unanimously denied the proposal because current offerings did not meet community needs at this time. The ordinance was first discussed in July when Abbey McLaren proposed it as an alternative to approving individual resolutions for events. Karla Hargett reported that a large majority of the Tifton Merchants Association members supported founding the district. The council reviewed the item again at a workshop earlier this month before making their final decision. The proposed district would allow residents to purchase alcohol from select downtown businesses and carry it around the area. Community businesses expressed support for the initiative, viewing it as a major step forward for Downtown Tifton. Dr. Jerry Baker and San Taylor are competing for a District 6 seat on the Tift County Schools Board of Education. San Taylor plans to focus on improving school policies and structure rather than switching out staff or schedules. Dr. Baker brings over thirty years of experience in education to the race against Taylor.
