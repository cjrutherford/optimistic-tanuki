Early Access

Help Shape a People-Centered Network

Our Privacy Standard

Private-community posts should stay within that community. In public communities, wider visibility should require the author to choose a public audience. These protections are still being validated.

Your Feed and Explore

The primary feed is intended for your chosen relationships and communities. Explore is separate; feed ordering is still being validated during early access.

Optional Communities

Communities bring people together around a shared interest. Community workflows are available to try while we test them.

Posts and Images

Try feed posts and images during early access. Publishing and uploads are still being tested.

Different Ways to Talk

Feed updates, messages, and the main forum make room for different kinds of conversation. Messaging and forum workflows are still being tested.

An Early-Stage Invitation

Help us test a network built around people and shared interests. This is early access, and the public-release checks are in progress.
