I enjoy understanding how complicated systems fit together, identifying where they become fragile or inefficient, and finding ways to make them simpler and more dependable.

Outside traditional engineering roles, I build software aimed at giving people more control over their digital lives. I believe useful technology should expand what people can do rather than leave them dependent on systems they cannot understand or maintain.

That includes helping people who are not technical use technology with confidence, and publishing open-source tools. My independent work lives at Hopeful Aspirations Industries.
