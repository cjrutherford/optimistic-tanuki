• Your words are published as yours, under whatever handle you choose, and are never rewritten.
  • Everything is reviewed automatically before it appears, and every decision comes with its reason. A report that alleges wrongdoing, names a private individual, or mentions a child waits for evidence rather than being published on one account.
  • There is no human appeal. When the automated review gets it wrong, the only recourse is more evidence.
  • Reposting a news article is refused automatically. Quoting a paragraph with credit is fine.
  • If you have an interest in the matter — you applied for the permit, you work for the department — say so. It does not disqualify your report. Hiding it would.
  • A briefing quotes almost nothing that is sent. Only an officially confirmed account, or a report that independent people corroborated and a later record bore out, is ever quoted. Everything else lives on the town's community page, labeled as what it is.
