How Towne Square works

Find places that matter to you

Browse available places, public community posts, and listings without an account. Create an account when you are ready to participate.

Local communities, classifieds, and conversation

Cities, towns, and neighborhoods

Find an available place, explore its communities, and read their public posts. Community membership lets you take part.

Local classifieds

Explore local listings, post a classified, and contact someone about a listing through messages.

Messages and community chat

Send direct messages, or use community chat once your membership is approved.
